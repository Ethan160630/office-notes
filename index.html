01一句话概括

Meeting Notes Recorder 是一个在本机完成录音与转写的会议记录工具。默认情况下，你的录音和转写文字只保存在这台 iPhone 上，开发者没有服务器，也拿不到你的内容。

我们没有账号系统、没有广告 SDK、没有行为分析 SDK。App 不会读取你的通讯录、照片、定位。

02我们处理哪些数据

数据用途存放位置
录音音频生成转写文字与纪要本机 App 沙盒目录
转写文字、纪要会议库、搜索、导出本机 App 沙盒目录
偏好设置记住识别方式、语言等本机（UserDefaults）

以上数据均不回传开发者，也不与你的身份关联。

03语音识别怎么走

默认：使用 Apple 系统提供的端侧语音识别（Speech framework）。音频在设备上处理，除非系统本身需要联网校准，此时由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。

可选：你可以在「设置 → 识别」里填入自己的第三方语音识别服务地址与密钥。一旦填写，音频或文字会被发送到你指定的那个服务，由该服务商按其自身条款处理；开发者不经手、也无法访问这部分数据。

App 出厂不包含任何第三方密钥，也不会替你选择服务商。是否启用云端识别，完全由你决定，随时可以关掉并清除密钥。

04权限说明

  • 麦克风：仅在你按下「开始录音」到「停止」之间使用。
  • 语音识别：把录音转成文字。
  • 蓝牙：连接蓝牙耳机录音时接收其麦克风声音。
  • 后台音频：锁屏或切换 App 时保持正在进行的录音不中断；不用于后台播放。

所有权限都可以在 iOS「设置 → 办公助理」中随时关闭。

05数据保留与删除

录音与文字保存在本机，保留多久由你决定。删除方式：

  • 在会议详情页删除单条会议（会同时删除对应音频文件）；
  • 在会议列表使用批量删除；
  • 卸载 App 会删除 App 沙盒内的全部数据。

删除后无法恢复，请自行导出需要长期保存的纪要。

06共享与披露

我们不出售、不出租、不交换你的数据。只有在下列情形才可能涉及第三方：

  • 你自己配置了第三方语音识别或 AI 服务（见第 03 节）；
  • 你主动使用系统分享导出文件；
  • 法律要求或有权机关依法调取——但我们本地无服务器，通常无从提供。

01Summary

Meeting Notes Recorder records and transcribes meetings on your device. By default, audio and transcripts stay in this iPhone's app container. The developer operates no server and cannot access your content.

No account system, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK. The app does not access your contacts, photos, or location.

02Data we process

DataPurposeWhere it lives
Audio recordingsProducing transcripts and summariesApp sandbox on device
Transcripts, summariesMeeting library, search, exportApp sandbox on device
PreferencesRemembering recognition mode, languageOn device (UserDefaults)

None of this is sent to the developer, and none is linked to your identity.

03How speech recognition works

Default: Apple's on-device speech framework. Audio is processed on the device, except where iOS itself performs a network-assisted check, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Optional: You may enter your own third-party transcription endpoint and key under Settings → Recognition. Once you do, audio or text is sent to that service and handled under its terms. The developer does not operate, proxy, or observe that traffic.

The app ships with no third-party key and picks no vendor for you. Cloud transcription is entirely your choice and can be switched off at any time.

04Permissions

  • Microphone — active only between pressing Start and Stop.
  • Speech recognition — turning recordings into text.
  • Bluetooth — receiving audio from Bluetooth headset microphones.
  • Background audio — keeping an in-progress recording alive when the screen locks or you switch apps; never used for background playback.

All permissions can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → 办公助理.

05Retention and deletion

Recordings and transcripts stay on your device for as long as you keep them. To delete:

  • Delete a meeting from its detail screen (its audio file is removed too);
  • Use batch delete in the meeting list;
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything in its container.

Deletion is permanent. Export anything you need to keep.

06Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Third parties are involved only when:

  • you configure your own transcription or AI service (see section 03);
  • you explicitly share an exported file through iOS;
  • required by law — though with no server of ours, there is normally nothing to produce.

07Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
